Case 001: The Silent Generator

A 250 kVA diesel generator runs normally without load, then overheats and shuts down after sustained operation. The previous technician replaced the thermostat. The fault remains.

MachineIndustrial diesel generator · 6-cylinder · liquid cooled · 1500 RPM
Reported symptomCoolant temperature climbs steadily during high-load operation. Automatic shutdown occurs near 112°C.
Known historyThermostat replaced yesterday. No visible coolant leak. Radiator exterior appears clean. Engine runs normally at idle.
Your objective: identify the cause using evidence, not guesswork. Replacing parts without a diagnosis reduces your investigation rating.

What you know

The engine produces more waste heat as load rises. The cooling system must move enough coolant through the engine and radiator to carry that heat away.
